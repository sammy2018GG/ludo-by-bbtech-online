Rules in this match

  • Roll two dice each turn. Each die is a separate move: tap a die to choose it first, then tap a token.
  • A 6 on either die brings a token out of base, and a 6 gives you an extra roll once your moves are done.
  • Three sixes in a row forfeits your turn.
  • Landing on a rival captures it, except on safe squares (marked with a star).
  • Tokens travel clockwise, turn into your home lane, and must land exactly on the centre to finish.
  • You must move when a legal move exists.
  • The match ends when all but one player have finished.

Play online with family

Play with family on your own phones, each in your own home or the same room. One person creates a room and shares the 4-digit code or link. Everyone else joins with that code, and the host starts the game when all seats are filled. The server rolls the dice and checks every move, so nobody can cheat.
